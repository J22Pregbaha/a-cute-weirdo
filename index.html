Hey you 👋🏾,

Welcome to the internet living room of yours truly, Joshua Pregbaha, a software engineer by day, tired man at night and a cute weirdo all day long.

I love Jesus, yoghurt and basketball, and I was very excited when I learnt that just like cars come with spare tyres, shirts come with spare buttons.

I can't tell you how glad I am that you're here. To entertain you, I've curated my wacky fun thoughts on Overflowing Thoughts, a blog I created for myself and a few other writers to express our creativity unhinged.

You can also enjoy some of the work I've written in fiction and poetry that has been published on blogs and online literary magazines.
